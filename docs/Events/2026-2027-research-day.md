# 2025-2026 Annual Research Day [University of Southampton Business School, 20/11/2026]

**Date:** Friday, November 20, 2026

**Time:** 9:30 AM - 5:10 PM GMT, with optional Pub Dinner afterwards

**Location:** University of Southampton Business School

**Details:**

The community traveled down to the coastal town of Southampton for the 2026-2027 Annual Research Day of the London+ Qualitative Community, hosted at the University of Southampton Business School.

This fourth edition, following three successful previous events, brought together qualitative researchers from across the UK for qualitative methodology learnings and discussions, networking, and research discussions.

The day included a methods workshop led by Kevin Corley (ICBS) and Mina Beigi (USBS), a speed networking event, research presentations and roundtables, and a panel discussion. We ended with a big dinner at a local pub, with an impressive amount of attendees continuing the conversations beyond the university walls!

Thank you all for your wonderful engagement--we are looking forward to seeing all of you, and more, at next year's L+QC Research Day!
